## Overview

Official personal portfolio and institutional archive of **Shakil Ilhan**, Civil Engineering undergraduate at **Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)**, **Founder & Founding Organizer** of the **American Concrete Institute (ACI) KUET Student Chapter**, **CFO & Co-Founder** of **Draft and Deploy Co.**, and **Senior Executive (Operations & Logistics)** at the **KUET Business & Entrepreneurship Club (KBEC)**.

- **Zero-Build High-Performance Frontend:** Pure semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 custom properties, and modular ES6 JavaScript.
- **Smooth Physics & Motion:** Powered by Lenis smooth scrolling and custom spring-physics displacement.
- **Responsive Across Viewports:** Adaptive rem grid scaling across mobile, tablet, laptop, and 4K displays.
- **Structured Data & SEO:** Schema.org JSON-LD (`Person` & `WebSite`), Open Graph metadata, sitemap, and verified Google Search Console indexing.
